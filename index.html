The weekly brief

One account brief, ready when you ask for it.

Preprocessed weekly and retrieved in Claude through the Alakai connector and skills. The latest brief and recent history are available immediately after setup.

Promotions

Offers that ended in the prior week, started, or remained active, with own-brand and competitor overlap by banner.

Price cascade

Regular price plus verified loyalty, multi-buy, store coupon and digital coupon conditions. Continuing offers carry forward; discounts combine only when stacking is verified.

Shelf prices

Regular shelf-price changes for the configured brands and banners, with comparable observations so a missing observation is never read as no change.

Excel source

The underlying store-week-GTIN observations as a downloadable .xlsx, including the fields needed to reproduce the banner-level read-out.

Briefs show observed prices and offers. Discounts are attributed to what was observed; trade funding, sales lift, margin and promotion ROI need evidence beyond observed price data and are not inferred.

How it works

From signup to your first brief in about ten minutes.

  1. Create your account and choose a retailer

    Select a supported retailer and up to three of its banners. You see the available geography, observation dates and coverage before you proceed.

  2. Set your watchlist

    Pick one internal brand and confirm three competitors. Alakai suggests competitors where it can. Edit any time within your plan limits.

  3. Connect Alakai to Claude

    A short guided setup adds the Alakai connector and skills to your personal Claude account.

  4. Retrieve your brief

    Ask Claude for this week's brief. Read it, ask follow-up questions, and download the Excel source file.

  5. Subscribe when you are ready

    Choose Basic or Pro, monthly or annual, through Stripe. Billing, plan changes and cancellation are self-service.

Works inside the Claude account you already use

Alakai is delivered through a Model Context Protocol connector and skills in a personal Claude account, so your brief lives where you already ask questions. There is no new dashboard to learn and no customer data to integrate.

Claude access is separate from your Alakai subscription. The trial demonstrates the complete Basic workflow, and access ends after 30 days unless you subscribe.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need to connect my own sales or planning data?

No. Setup needs only your retailer, banners and brands. Alakai works from observed shelf data. You can connect the Excel source file to your own performance analysis if you want to.

What do I need on the Claude side?

A personal Claude account that supports connectors. The guided setup adds the Alakai connector and its skills. Claude access is billed separately by its provider and is not included in an Alakai plan.

Which retailers and brands are covered?

Alakai observes 45 grocery banners today and adds more each month. When you create your account you see the supported retailers, banners, geography and observation dates before you commit. If your retailer or category is not yet covered, book a call and we will tell you when it will be.

How current is the data?

Briefs refresh on a consistent weekly schedule and each one shows its latest observation date, observed store counts and geography. Exact duration or overlap claims use explicit offer dates; otherwise the brief describes the observed weeks.

What happens when the trial ends?

Access ends after 30 days unless you subscribe. The expiry message links directly to checkout, where you choose Basic or Pro and monthly or annual billing. You can change plans or cancel yourself at any time.

Can my whole team use one subscription?

Plans are per subscriber, each with one saved watchlist. Team administration is not part of this release. For several people or several retailers, the Enterprise route sets an agreed scope.
